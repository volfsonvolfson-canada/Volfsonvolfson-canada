Activities and Practices at Back to Base

Where nature and quiet become part of your practice

Invitation to Collaborate

Back to Base welcomes those who create transformative practices and help people heal and restore. We are looking for: Program creators Yoga instructors Meditation teachers Massage therapists Reiki practitioners Acupuncturists Body-oriented specialists If you want to share your work in the quiet of the forest beside a mountain lake, we would be happy to collaborate with you. Just call or message us!

Our locations for your workshops

Cedar platforms & creek light — open gallery

Outdoor space with multi functional platforms

Just a few steps from the house, a winding path leads into the forest, where wooden platforms are hidden among tall trees. The air feels lighter here, the sound of the creek creates a natural meditation, and the soft light filtering through the canopy makes every practice deeper. It's an ideal spot for: Sunrise yoga Evening meditations Breathwork Any activity that benefits from a strong connection to nature

Gather here when the weather turns — look

Multifunctional indoor space

Inside the house, there is a spacious room with large windows filled with light, warmth, and a sense of comfort — perfect for group gatherings, mini-lectures, workshops, breathwork sessions, or yoga during cooler weather. And if you need a more intimate atmosphere or plan to use visual materials, the room can easily be darkened with blackout curtains.

Blanket-film nights & soft seats — peek in

Cozy mini home theatre

For presentations, educational films, documentaries, or shared viewing sessions, we offer a small but very cozy home theatre. Soft lighting, quality sound, and a calm environment help create a fully immersive experience.

Are you looking for a place to retreat or interested in joining a workshop?

Just send us a message with your preferences, and we will create a program tailored specifically for you!
